### Project Description

**GHOOGLE PHOTOS** is a parody AI-powered photo analysis system that begins as a professional-looking photo analysis platform and slowly becomes something much stranger. Upload a group photograph and let the AI analyze it — until it detects someone who shouldn't be there.

What begins as **5 subjects detected** becomes **6... then mysteriously returns to 5**.

Users can then choose between **LIGHT**, an absurd paranormal analysis experience, and **DARK**, an interactive horror experience where the system gradually becomes aware of the person using it.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Have you ever looked at a perfectly normal group photograph and wondered:

**"What if there was someone in the photo who wasn't supposed to be there?"**

Traditional photo-analysis systems only identify the people actually present.

GHOOGLE PHOTOS solves this completely unnecessary problem by detecting suspicious entities, paranormal activity, and potentially... **you.**

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

GHOOGLE PHOTOS uses a completely legitimate™ AI photo-analysis system to detect faces, analyze photographs, identify suspicious entities, and occasionally discover an extra person who apparently doesn't exist.

The system then gives the user two choices:

- **LIGHT** — A hilarious paranormal analysis filled with absurd classifications and ridiculous AI conclusions.
- **DARK** — An interactive horror experience where the environment changes, an entity appears, the system reacts to the user, and the AI slowly realizes that **the user is part of the analysis**.
